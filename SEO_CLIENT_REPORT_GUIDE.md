# SEO Client Report & Pricing Guide

## Pricing Structure

### One-Time Technical SEO Setup

| What's Included | Freelancer Rate |
|----------------|----------------|
| Meta tags, schema, sitemap, robots.txt, OG tags, structured data | ₹15,000 – ₹40,000 |
| WebP images, FAQ schema, breadcrumbs, Core Web Vitals | ₹10,000 – ₹25,000 |
| **Total one-time setup** | **₹25,000 – ₹65,000** |

### Monthly SEO Retainer (Ongoing)

| Service | Monthly Rate |
|---------|-------------|
| Blog content (4 posts/month) | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 |
| Search Console monitoring & reporting | ₹3,000 – ₹8,000 |
| Backlink building | ₹5,000 – ₹15,000 |
| **Total monthly retainer** | **₹10,000 – ₹30,000/month** |

### Pricing Formula

> **Your hourly rate × hours spent + value delivered**

- 15-20 hours SEO setup → charge at least ₹25,000 – ₹35,000
- Ongoing maintenance → ₹10,000 – ₹15,000/month minimum
- International clients (US/UK) → multiply rates by 3-5x

### Pricing Tips

- Don't charge too low — SEO has long-term business value (leads, sales)
- Show the deliverables — use SEO_IMPLEMENTATION.md as proof of work
- Charge separately for website build and SEO — they are different services

## What Results to Show the Client

### Phase 1: Immediate Deliverables (Day 1-7)

#### 1. Google PageSpeed Score
- Run the site on PageSpeed Insights (https://pagespeed.web.dev)
- Screenshot the Performance, SEO, Accessibility scores
- Show before SEO → after SEO improvement
- Target: 90+ SEO score

#### 2. Rich Results Test
- Test on Google Rich Results Test (https://search.google.com/test/rich-results)
- Show the structured data is valid (star ratings, pricing, business info)
- Screenshot: "Page is eligible for rich results"

#### 3. Mobile-Friendly Test
- Show the site passes Google's mobile-friendly check
- Screenshot the green checkmark

#### 4. Technical SEO Audit Report
- Use free tools like Lighthouse or SEMrush free audit
- Show before/after comparison
- Highlight: meta tags, sitemap, robots.txt, schema — all done

---

### Phase 2: Monthly Reports (Month 1-3)

Set up Google Search Console and Google Analytics, then share monthly.

#### 5. Search Console Report

| Metric | What to Show |
|--------|-------------|
| Impressions | How many times site appeared in Google |
| Clicks | How many people clicked |
| Average Position | Keyword ranking position |
| CTR | Click-through rate |
| Indexed Pages | How many pages Google has indexed |

#### 6. Keyword Rankings

Show a table like:

| Keyword | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---------|---------|---------|---------|
| online fitness coach India | Not ranked | #45 | #22 |
| NASM certified coach India | #80 | #35 | #18 |
| affordable fitness coaching | Not ranked | #50 | #30 |

#### 7. Traffic Growth

From Google Analytics:
- Organic visitors per month (graph going up)
- Top landing pages
- User location (India traffic)
- Bounce rate improvement

### Phase 3: Business Impact (Month 3-6)

#### 8. Lead Generation
- How many enquiries came from organic search
- WhatsApp clicks from Google search visitors
- Assessment form submissions from organic traffic

#### 9. Google Business Profile Stats
- Profile views
- Direction requests
- Phone calls from Google

## Key Advice

- Set realistic expectations — tell the client results take 2-3 months minimum
- Report monthly — even small improvements show progress
- Focus on business metrics — the client cares about leads and calls, not technical jargon
- Compare before/after — always show where they started vs where they are now
